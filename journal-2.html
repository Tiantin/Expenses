☁️ Upload workflow
Step 1 — Auto backup (set once, forget)
Google Photos → Settings → Backup → On. Photos upload on Wi-Fi. Nothing to do daily.
Step 2 — Move to Citipac album
Select today's site photos → Add to album → use this format:
CITIPAC_[BUILDING]_YYYYMMDD
Step 3 — Caption any issues
Tap photo → Edit → add description. Captions are searchable months later.
